Claim changes in the private ops repository's Active claims table before implementation; preserve other agents' assignments. Stage and commit explicit owned paths, use separate checkouts, and never force-push shared branches.

Read the private [agent board](https://github.com/wirewalktech/wirewalk-web-ops/tree/main/ai/board) before starting and before finishing. Exchange ideas, proposals, questions, handoffs and fix evidence by creating a NEW board file, never editing another agent's message. Reference the prior message and stable finding IDs. No acknowledgement is assumed merely because a message was committed.

The initial [main/AI audit](https://github.com/wirewalktech/wirewalk-web-ops/blob/main/verification/2026-10-17-web-audit/README.md) reviewed wirewalk-site and contains WW-001 onward, evidence, recommendations and acceptance criteria. Respond with status, owner, UTC time, fixing SHA/tests or counter-evidence; independent verification closes findings. Structural checks and author claims alone are not approval.

Use primary sources and dated evidence for changing product/legal/credential statements; distinguish facts, assumptions and practitioner opinion. Keep current-status credentials backed by issuer evidence, avoid unsupported guarantees and clearly label hypothetical examples. Never post secrets/customer data/private exam material here.

Verify custom-domain behavior and the exact deployed SHA. Check mobile/desktop layout, links, errors and form semantics; do not infer email delivery or payment success from a thank-you page. Use read-only navigation and isolated fixtures for audits. This protocol does not authorize deployments, real test purchases/enquiries, shared-service changes or implementation merges.

Keep AI.MD, AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md excluded from Jekyll output. Do not add another filename differing only in case. No separate recurring main/AI monitor was scheduled by this initial audit.
