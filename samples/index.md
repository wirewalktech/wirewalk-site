## A format you can assess before buying

These are fictional examples of the structure and level of explanation a deliverable can contain. They are not completed client reports or evidence of commissioned results. The accepted scope determines the tests and documents for a real engagement.

Each sample is readable in the browser and available as a Markdown download. No email address is required. [Read the procurement guide](/procurement/) or [see current engagement options](/order/) when you are ready.
