## Begin with the requirements that apply

Research and clinical systems can carry specific data-handling, access, retention and audit obligations. We identify the applicable requirements, the system boundary and the people responsible before choosing controls or assessment activities.

The service offers readiness review, full assessment and remediation as separate scopes. Existing areas of work include NIST SP 800-171, NIH controlled-access research, HIPAA requirements and export-control handling where applicable. Framework applicability and responsibilities are agreed for the engagement; a product setting or a review does not itself establish organizational certification or legal compliance.

## Follow controls through the working system

The assessment can examine storage, scheduler, identity and audit together. On a shared cluster, the boundary may include login access, scratch data, node images, job automation and mounts available to many users. I review how those pieces enforce the intended separation and what records can demonstrate it.

Findings identify the observation, its scope and why it matters. We distinguish a configured policy from tested behavior and record limitations where evidence is missing. Assessment work is performed under written authorization.

## Choose readiness, assessment or implementation

A readiness review establishes where the environment stands against the agreed framework and what closing the gaps would involve. A full assessment examines the agreed system in more depth, with the system security plan and POA&M where included in that scope.

A remediation program is a separate implementation engagement. It covers agreed controls and the evidence needed to review them afterward. That separation lets the team decide which work it can perform internally and where outside engineering would help.

## Protect the research while work proceeds

Access, handling rules and sensitive material are agreed before exchange. An NDA can be arranged, and the scope determines which tests may be performed without affecting research or availability. Production changes require the agreed maintenance and recovery process.
