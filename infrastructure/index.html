HPC and AI

Systems shaped around the research.

A useful system starts with understanding the work people need to do. I bring compute, storage, networks and scientific software together, and stay involved through the practical work of making them run well.

01

Cluster architecture and build-out

I help turn research needs into a working cluster: choosing hardware, planning capacity, installing systems and setting up the tools your team will use to manage them. My work includes Bright Cluster Manager, OpenHPC, HPCM and xCAT, across individual sites and larger environments.

02

InfiniBand and low latency fabric

When an analysis spans many machines, the connections between them matter. My experience spans SDR and DDR InfiniBand through EDR and HDR, along with a wide range of RoCE interconnects. I design, upgrade and troubleshoot these networks, using OpenSM and NVIDIA UFM and tracing problems through the switches, adapters and applications.

03

Parallel and scale-out storage

Research data needs room to grow and a dependable path to the people and programs using it. I build and tune parallel storage, plan data movement and recovery, and test it with the actual workload. Systems I work with include IBM Storage Scale / GPFS, Lustre, WEKA, VAST, PowerScale / Isilon and DDN.

04

GPU, CUDA and AI stacks

I build GPU environments for model training, inference and scientific computing, with particular experience in NVIDIA systems: A100, H100, DGX, CUDA, NVLink and NCCL. The work includes matching memory, storage and networking to the job, then making PyTorch, TensorFlow and container environments usable for the team.

05

Schedulers and workload management

A shared cluster needs priorities that make sense to the people using it. I configure Slurm, PBS Pro and LSF, including queues, GPU allocations, fair share and usage accounting. I also help teams move to a new scheduler while keeping their research workflows in view.

06

Cloud and hybrid HPC

I help you work out where cloud computing fits your research and budget, then build the environment and move the work. That includes AWS ParallelCluster, FSx for Lustre, EFA and Azure HPC, as well as systems that connect cloud resources with the equipment you already own.

Other areas

The everyday work that keeps research moving.

Researchers also need accounts that work, software they can use, data they can find and someone to turn to when a job fails. I work alongside the people supporting those needs, including the planning, maintenance and documentation that keep a system useful over time.

Enterprise IT and infrastructure

I look after Linux and UNIX systems, identity services, software environments and monitoring. I also help teams move from older platforms, taking care to understand the applications and working habits that depend on them. Tools include Ansible, Puppet, Prometheus and Grafana.

Infrastructure

Low latency networking

I design and troubleshoot the networks that connect researchers, instruments, compute and data. That includes campus networks and connections between sites, using Juniper, Arista, Cisco and Brocade platforms. I follow the path the research traffic actually takes to find where it slows down.

Network

Security, compliance and audit

I help teams build access controls, logging, patching and recovery into the way their systems are run. My experience includes research and clinical environments with specific data-handling and audit requirements. The detailed frameworks and responsibilities are described below.

Compliance

Data management and movement

I plan storage growth, migrations, backup and archive so people can keep working with their data as projects develop. That includes shared filesystems, snapshots, replication, tape and transfers between sites, with attention to access, retention and recovery.

Storage
