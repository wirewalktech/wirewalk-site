## Add an independent engineering perspective

I work across compute, GPU software, interconnects, storage and the workloads that use them. A partner engagement can bring that perspective into a design review, difficult performance investigation, delivery acceptance or operational handover.

## Where collaboration can help

- **Server and GPU integrators:** workload qualification, topology review, acceptance evidence and an independent readout.
- **Storage and network teams:** cross-layer investigations where an application symptom spans more than one product.
- **Research computing groups:** additional engineering capacity for an agreed build, upgrade or difficult diagnosis.
- **Existing IT service providers:** a defined specialist work package with documented interfaces and handover.

## Define ownership at the beginning

Agree who communicates with the research team, who authorizes changes, which evidence can be shared and who owns the supported configuration. The written scope identifies deliverables, access, escalation and any retesting. Commercial or referral arrangements are agreed separately; this page promises no exclusivity, commission or vendor endorsement.
